# This data is used through the Math 430 lectures, labs, and homeworks.

## Dataset: Typing.csv

Cleaned data from:
[Scientific Research and Methodology textbook](https://bookdown.org/pkaldunn/SRM-Textbook/AppendixDataSets.html)

The Typing dataset contains four variables: typing speed (mTS), typing accuracy (mAcc), age (Age), and sex (Sex) for 1,301 students (Pinet et al. 2022).

Pinet, Svetlana, Christelle Zielinski, F.-Xavier Alario, and Marieke Longcamp. 2022. “Typing Expertise in a Large Student Population.” Cognitive Research: Principles and Implications 7 (1): 77.


## Dataset: OSA.csv

Cleaned data from: 
[Scientific Research and Methodology textbook](https://bookdown.org/pkaldunn/SRM-Textbook/AppendixDataSets.html)

de Carvalho et al. (2020) studied obstructive sleep apnoea (OSA) in  60 adults with Down Syndrome. 
The response variable y is OSA severity. 
The explanatory variable x is the average number of episodes of sleep disruption (according to specific criteria) per hour of sleep, the Respiratory Event Index (REI).

de Carvalho, Anderson Albuquerque, Fabio Ferreira Amorim, Levy Aniceto Santana, Karlo Jozefo Quadros de Almeida, Alfredo Nicodemos Cruz Santana, and Francisco de Assis Rocha Neves. 2020. “STOP-Bang Questionnaire Should Be Used in All Adults with Down Syndrome to Screen for Moderate to Severe Obstructive Sleep Apnea.” PLOS ONE 15 (5): e0232596.
